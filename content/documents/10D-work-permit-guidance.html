Youth Employment Permits and Work Hours

PFPS Student Services Guidance | Revised 2024

Permits. Students under 18 need a youth employment permit before starting a job. Permits are issued through the school district, and a parent or guardian signs the application.

Hours for 16- and 17-year-olds. Under Massachusetts law (M.G.L. c. 149), 16- and 17-year-olds may not work later than 10 PM on a night before a school day, and may not work more than 48 hours a week.

When work affects school. If a job appears to be affecting a student’s attendance, health or learning, the school may meet with the student and family to review the permit. The goal is to help the student keep a job that fits the law and their school day, not to take the job away.

Academic Intervention & Support Referral Policy

Policy IHBA: Academic Support Referrals

Section 3: 'Upon identification of a skill gap through diagnostic assessment, the School Counselor shall schedule a Support Plan meeting with the student's teacher(s) and family within ten (10) school days of referral.'

Section 4: 'Failure to convene within the specified window requires immediate escalation to the Assistant Principal for scheduling.'
